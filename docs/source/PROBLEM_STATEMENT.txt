Problem Statement
    Persons with disabilities (PwDs) often face significant barriers in acquiring workplace communication skills 
    due to limited access to inclusive training platforms. Traditional communication training programs are 
    generally designed for the broader population and do not adequately address the diverse accessibility 
    needs of individuals with visual, hearing, speech, cognitive, or learning disabilities.

Development Guidelines
    Develop an AI-assisted workplace communication training application that provides an 
    accessible, personalized, and interactive learning environment for persons with disabilities. 
    The application should leverage Generative AI, Natural Language Processing (NLP), 
    Speech Technologies, and Assistive AI to simulate real-world workplace communication scenarios, 
    evaluate users' communication skills, and provide adaptive feedback tailored to their 
    individual abilities and learning needs.

    Features
    •
    Database of approximately 200 workplace communication words and phrases
    •
    Speech, pronunciation, and fluency analysis
    •
    AI-assisted contextual learning
    •
    Social stories for workplace adaptation
    •
    Interactive simulations of workplace communication scenarios
    •
    Personalized learning recommendations
    •
    Mock interview and HR interaction simulator
    •
    Gamified learning modules with badges and achievements
    •
    Progress dashboard for learners, trainers, and institutions
    Expected Solution
    ✓
    Improve workplace communication skills among persons with disabilities
    ✓
    Enhance confidence during interviews and workplace interactions
    ✓
    Increase employability and workplace readiness
    ✓
    Provide inclusive, accessible, and personalized AI-based communication training
    ✓
    Enable institutions and employers to monitor communication competency through analytics
